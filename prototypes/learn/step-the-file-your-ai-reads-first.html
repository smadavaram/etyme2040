Part 1 · Watch

Milo gets a note

Six short scenes. Press Next when you are ready. Nothing plays by itself.

1 · Two customers, one question

Two people ask Milo, the helper at Maple Mart, the same thing. Milo has no notes.

Monday“My order is late.” → “Apologies for the inconvenience. Deliveries may take additional time.”
Tuesday“My order is late.” → “Oh no! We’ll give you your money back right now!”
Same question. Two different answers. One of them promised money nobody approved.

2 · Why it happens

Milo starts each chat knowing nothing about how Maple Mart likes things done. It fills the gap with a guess. A guess changes from day to day.

No notes
→
A new guess each time

3 · The shop writes a note

Ana, who runs the shop, writes down how she wants customers answered. She saves it as a plain text file.

milo.md# How Milo answers - Greet the customer by name. - Keep answers short. - Never promise money back. Ask Ana.

4 · What “md” means

The name ends in .md. That stands for Markdown: plain text with a few simple marks. A person can read it. So can an AI.

The mark# at the start of a line
→
It meansThis line is a heading
The mark- at the start of a line
→
It meansThis line is one item in a list

5 · Milo reads it first, every time

Before Milo answers anybody, it reads the file from top to bottom. Then it reads the customer’s message.

1 · Read milo.md
→
2 · Read the question
→
3 · Answer

6 · Same question, same answer

Now every customer gets the answer Ana wanted. And the money question goes to a person.

Any day“My order is late.” → “Hi Sam. Your order is one day late. I have asked Ana about your money.”
To change how Milo behaves, Ana edits one file. She does not retrain anything.
