# 135. "AI from Zero in 3 Hours" is Etyme's course — 2026-11-28

The owner's rulings of 2026-11-27 and 28, given directly in session. They
replace the parts of records 133 and 134 that put the first course on
Tapin.

## What the course is for

The owner: "Goal here is to attract as many users as possible by
converting 3 hr training into an animation presented, based step by step
training with questionnaire and workbook based learning. At end of each
section will be a real world demo example - we are targeting kids,
starting out, people from non-it and IT - we can use this people to take
Etyme forward." And: "I am now focussed on viral training that everybody
in the world can start tomorrow."

## Rulings

1. **Etyme owns the course, at Etyme's own address.** Tapin is one
   supplier among many and adopts the course like any other. This keeps
   Etyme independent of its own agency. The course sits at its own
   address with one door from Etyme's home page; it is never the home
   page's headline, which still speaks to the client.
2. **The name is "AI from Zero in 3 Hours".** The owner chose it on
   2026-11-28. Two earlier names were set aside because each borrowed a
   name that belongs to somebody else.
3. **The format.** Each step is an animated, step-by-step presentation,
   then something to try, a questionnaire and a workbook entry. Each of
   the six sections closes with a real-world demo. Three levels: Simple,
   Medium, Complex. Four audiences: kids, starting out, non-IT, IT.
4. **The base is the owner's own course file**: 33 steps, 9 chapters in 3
   levels, 4 paths, a lab per step, 6 quizzes, 3 projects and a 13-step
   walkthrough. The lessons written on 2026-11-27 and the first draft
   before them are retired as the base.
5. **The training is converted whole**, from its full transcript and its
   frames, never from a summary. Every idea, example and step is carried;
   every sentence is rewritten in the course's own words. Nothing of the
   source is named or shown.
6. **Length.** The owner: "6-9hrs is minimum expected", then the name's
   "in 3 Hours". The lead's reading, not yet confirmed by the owner:
   about 3 hours to watch all 33 steps, 6 to 9 hours with the labs,
   questionnaires, workbook, demos and projects.

7. **A 34th step, our own: "The file your AI reads first".** The owner
   asked whether the course teaches what an md file is and how it helps.
   It teaches skills, roles and pinned instructions, and never names the
   file. The owner: "Yes add it as its own step." It sits in the Simple
   level after skills. It is drawn whole as the first step in the new
   format: `prototypes/learn/step-the-file-your-ai-reads-first.html`.
