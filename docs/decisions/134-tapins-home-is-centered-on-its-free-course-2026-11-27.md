# 134. Tapin's home is centered on its free course — 2026-11-27

Rulings relayed by Pax (architect) on 2026-11-27, on the owner's word,
while the Tapin home page and the first course were being built.

## The home page

- Tapin's home page is centered on "AI for Everyone". The free course
  gateway is the hero, not a side link. The page's parts are AI for
  Everyone (the center), Career Bench, and Pods. The bench and pods are
  reached from the course, because the course feeds both: completions
  become bench proof, and bench proof is what pods sell.
- Pax built the reference page (`tapin-homepage-ai-centric-2026-11-27.html`,
  in Pax's source pack). The product page follows it: the same sections
  in the same order, the same tokens, the same voice. Where an earlier
  build differs, the reference wins. The on-ramp animation is Pax's
  pattern and is reproduced, not replaced.
- Tapin's tokens: ink `#0a1c2c`, ink-2 `#0f2a40`, ink-3 `#14334d`, teal
  `#0db5a3`, teal-dark `#078f81`, teal-soft `#e0f5f1`, navy-text
  `#eaf2f7`, navy-muted `#a9bfce`. Headings Sora, body Manrope. Never
  Etyme's purple.
- The hero picture and the course's animations are Pax's to supply. A
  page is built with a slot. Nobody generates a substitute. A graphic
  with no slot is flagged to Pax.
- "Pod" stands on the home page's approved offer line. Record 120's
  "project and team, never pod" governs client documents: proposals,
  contracts, statements of work.
- The course link stays on the client-facing door. Record 69 governs any
  claim that Tapin sells training as a service; a door into a free
  course is not that claim. It is worded as an entry, never as a service.

## Pax's answers to seven questions

1. A self-paced public course earns a completion: on the record, badged,
   shareable. A certification is only what an assessor or a cohort's
   trainer issued. The credential page labels each truthfully.
2. The Kids/student track is public and read-only. A minor does not sign
   in, holds no record and shares nothing. Progress stays in the browser.
3. A signed-out learner's progress stays in the browser. On sign-in it
   carries over to the record. That carry-over is the join conversion.
4. Tapin's branding is approved as supplied. The Train to bench switch is
   the Owner's at launch, never an agent's. Everything is built safe with
   the switch off.
5. Proof on a public page is counts and skills from the record. A count
   is printed only at 10 or more; below that, the skill or badge alone.
6. The layout is the reference page above.
7. This course is free and public, the marketing gateway. Record 56's
   "reached by invitation" stays the default for every other course.

## What was built

- A fourth layout on the one engine, "Course first", and a font pairing,
  "Sora headlines, Manrope body". Any firm may pick either.
- `Company.siteHome` holds the layout's palette, picture and words. One
  reader, `courseHomeOf`, decides whether it is drawn: the layout chosen,
  the Owner's yes covering the two brand colors the palette stands on,
  every word and color present. Otherwise the standing page is drawn.
- The page is drawn only while the firm's course site is on, so no link
  leads nowhere.
- Tapin's content is written to Tapin's record by `scripts/tapin-home.ts`,
  run by a person. It gives no yes and switches nothing.
- `publicCount` holds the floor of 10 for the page and for what a
  buyer's own assistant reads.
